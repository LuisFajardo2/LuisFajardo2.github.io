Cute Cat Calculator

Privacy Policy.

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Pandy Brandy provides Cute Cat Calculator and is responsible for this policy. We designed the app to work with as little data as possible.

Calculations and accounts

Numbers you enter and results are processed locally on your device. The app does not create accounts, send your calculations to Pandy Brandy, or keep a permanent calculation history.

Information stored on your device

The app locally stores your selected style, sound volume, cookie balance and refill timing, cat companion activities and accessories, age group, and a random identifier for the latest reward to prevent duplicates. Android may also temporarily retain the visible calculation to restore the screen. You can remove this information by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Clipboard

The app writes a result to the system clipboard only when you choose to copy it. It does not read clipboard contents.

Advertising and external providers

The app offers optional Google AdMob rewarded ads in exchange for cookies. You do not need to watch an ad to use the calculator. Google Mobile Ads may collect and share the IP address—which can be used to estimate a general location—app and advertising interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. This data is encrypted in transit. The app excludes the Android advertising ID permission, but Google may process other technical identifiers. We do not send your calculations to Google.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) displays and stores applicable privacy choices. When required, you can reopen them from Settings, Advertising privacy. The app requests non-personalized ads and initializes the advertising SDK only after checking consent status. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Age, children, and families

A neutral age screen appears before an advertising request. The entered date is used temporarily to determine an age group; it is not saved or sent. Only the resulting group is stored locally. For people under 18 and people whose age is unknown, the ad request is marked for child-directed treatment, content is limited to a G rating, and non-personalized ads are requested. The app does not request a name, email address, precise location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

This website

This site uses no forms, analytics cookies, or first-party trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security purposes under its own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

We will publish changes here and show the new update date.
